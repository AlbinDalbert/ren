In the age before the Imperium, the world was ruled by the Ten Thousand Voices—calling for justice, order, and peace. But the voices grew louder and more dissonant. The land trembled beneath the weight of their endless quarrels. The air grew thick with unrest, and the people, weary from the constant bickering, watched helplessly as cities cracked into rubble, rivers flooded, and violence rose and the uncontained fire within the people let loose.

They burned themse§lves, the ten thousand voices had poisoned the air and they burned themselves and took the world with them.

With only the purest essence of humanity remaining, The Black Sand. As far as the eye could see. Stillness and silence loud as thunder stretched over the land as the Black Sand settled on the ground, each grain holding a fragment of humanity's lost wisdom.

Fire! Molten rock spewed forth from the depths of the earth in a circle around the land. The molten rock flowed across the blackness, and where it touched the Black Sand, something miraculous occurred. The Black Sand, remnants of humanity's essence, transformed the crude stone into something divine—pure, perfected iron! The Sacred Iron followed channels drawn by the Black Sand itself, each stream originating from a different corner of the land. All converged into a single form.

Arenatus! A Titan of The Sacred Iron—purified by, and cast in a mold of the Black Sand!

Arenatus rose from his mold, and the few mortals who had survived the collapse were drawn to him in their desperation. Through the Sacred Metal—forged in the collapse and purified by the essence of the Ten Thousand Voices—he crafted with his own hands a new path, a new order: The Imperium—Arenatus: The first Consul

Thus dawned the age of iron and order. Yet the voices of the people endured. Rather than being silenced, they were refined and tempered—just as the Sacred Iron itself had been. Through this, Arenatus created The Senate, a chamber where the common people could make their voices heard. A bridge, linking common folk to the divine order of the Imperium. The voice now with renewed purpose, guided by the iron’s steady hand, rather than lost to chaos.

For centuries, Arenatus ruled the Imperium. No one knew how old a Titan could become—he had outlived many generations—but he grew weaker. As he realized his immortality was not absolute, he founded the Sentire Prime, the highest court. This entity had one singular purpose: to protect the Imperium from chaos and corruption, ensuring his Will would live on long after his demise.
